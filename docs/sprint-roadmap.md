HOPE, Inc. ERP — Sprint Roadmap
Project Schedule
The HOPE, Inc. ERP project is planned across four sprints over eight weeks. Each sprint covers two weeks.

Sprint 1 — Weeks 1–2: Foundation and Access Control
Main focus: Establish the project foundation, database, authentication, and access-control groundwork.

Planned work:
Set up the application and repository foundation.
Prepare database tables, migrations, and sample data.
Implement user registration, login, logout, and password setup/reset.
Establish User Management, including user approval and account administration.
Prepare roles, module rights, and per-action permissions.
Establish audit logging and the main application navigation.

Module coverage: User Management groundwork begins in Sprint 1. The other business modules receive their access-control and database groundwork where needed.

Sprint 2 — Weeks 3–4: Customers and Products
Main focus: Build the customer and product management features.

Planned work:
Customer list, search, and filtering.
Customer creation, editing, soft deletion, and restoration.
Product list, search, and filtering.
Product creation, editing, soft deletion, and restoration.
Product price history, including effective dates.
Permission checks for customer and product actions.

Modules: Customers and Products.
Sprint 3 — Weeks 5–6: Human Resources
Main focus: Build the Human Resources features.

Planned work:
Department and job management.
Employee records and profiles.
Employee job history, including effective dates and salary information.
Appropriate permission checks for HR information.

Module: Human Resources.

Sprint 4 — Weeks 7–8: Sales and Reports
Main focus: Complete sales transactions and reporting features.

Planned work:
Sales transaction creation and management.
Sale detail line items and product price lookup based on the sales date.
Recording payments and viewing balances.
Sales soft deletion and restoration.
Reports, including product, employee, and top customer/product/sales reports.
Report permissions and print/save-as-PDF features.
Final integration and testing.

Modules: Sales and Reports.

Sprint Planning Notes
The project remains within the eight-week schedule.
Each sprint covers two weeks.
User Management and access rights begin during Sprint 1 and support the other modules.
Reports are included in Sprint 4.
Sprint work should be tracked using GitHub Issues and the GitHub Project board.
Stories should have clear acceptance criteria before being treated as ready for development.
